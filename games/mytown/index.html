🏗️ In My Town
🚗
🏘️ Places — drag or tap to build
✏️ Build your town to make sentences!

How to play

  1. 1 Drag a place, or tap it then tap an empty spot.
  2. 2 Fill your town with places! You can add lots of the same place.
  3. 3 Tap a building in your town to remove it.
  4. 4 Read the sentences and say them out loud!

Try it: "There is a park." "There are two hospitals."
