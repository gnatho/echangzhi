Snakes & Ladders - Question Topics Reference
=============================================

Level 1 - Easy: Basic vocabulary, opposites, colors, counting, simple categories
Level 2 - Basic: Spelling, synonyms/antonyms, rhyming, plurals, homophones
Level 3 - Intermediate: Grammar basics (nouns, verbs, adjectives), punctuation, articles
Level 4 - Challenging: Reading comprehension, word forms, figurative language, prefixes/suffixes
Level 5 - Hard: Commonly confused words, etymology, word origins, advanced vocabulary
Level 6 - Expert: Complex grammar (subjunctive, gerunds, modifiers), nuanced vocabulary
Level 7 - Master: Linguistic concepts, etymology deep dives, discourse analysis, rare words

Curriculum Alignment:
- Levels 1-2: Ages 5-7 (KS1)
- Levels 3-4: Ages 7-9 (KS2 Lower)
- Levels 5-6: Ages 9-11 (KS2 Upper)
- Level 7: Ages 11+ (KS3 Extension)
